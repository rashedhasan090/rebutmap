\section{Introduction}
Agents call tools in long sequences, and failures are hard to trace back to their cause.

\section{Related Work}
Tracing systems for distributed services record spans and parent links~\cite{dapper}.
Unlike prior tracing systems, our approach reconstructs causal links between tool calls offline,
without instrumenting the agent.
